Agents Holding Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Agents Holding is a developer tool that helps users coordinate AI-assisted work with services such as Jira, Confluence, Git providers, and local development tools.

Information we access

When you connect an integration, Agents Holding accesses only the data permitted by the scopes you approve. For Atlassian, this may include your account identity, accessible sites, Jira issues, comments, projects, boards, sprints, and related work-management data needed for the requested operation.

How we use information

We use connected-service data to perform the actions you request, such as reading or updating Jira work items and coordinating local agent workflows. We do not sell personal information or use connected-service content for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored using the operating system credential store where supported. Access tokens are exchanged through our server and are not displayed in the terminal. We use reasonable technical safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data is sent only to the connected service and infrastructure required to provide the feature you explicitly use, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke access from your Atlassian account or disconnect the integration with Agents Holding. Local credentials can be removed with the relevant logout command. Contact us if you need help removing server-side connection records.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact phuongnamp99@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated when the product or its integrations change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
